Record your first hit

  1. Connect your webcam and point it at the target so the shooting area is visible in the preview.
  2. Open Camera and select the webcam. If it is missing from the list, click Refresh webcams. Choose a resolution and frame rate if the camera mode list is available.
  3. Open Weapon and select the profile for your setup.
  4. Open Shooting and click Reset hit count if you want to start from zero.
  5. Fire a test pulse at the target. A registered hit increases the Hits counter. If the counter stays at zero, follow the missed hits checklist.

Settings reference

Shooting

Reset hit count sets the Hits counter to zero.

Camera

Select a webcam, choose an available resolution and frame rate, and use the flip selector to orient the preview. Adjust brightness, contrast, and saturation while checking the preview. Click Save webcam settings to keep your changes.

Detection

Simple
Looks for the brightest pixels that meet the detection threshold.
Blob
Uses the center of the largest connected bright region that meets the minimum area.
Sensitivity
Increase sensitivity to pick up dimmer spots. Decrease it if bright areas in the scene are counted as hits.
Show flares
Highlights bright regions that meet the detection threshold so you can see which parts of the image may affect detection.

Click Save detection settings to keep your changes.

Target

Target settings are currently unavailable.

Weapon

Trigger type
FirstSpot can count a spot again once the detection interval expires. NewSpot requires the spot to disappear and reappear after the interval before it can count again. External Event counts one hit per accepted press of T while the main window is active, provided a frame captured while T is held contains a laser spot. The detection interval starts with each accepted press, even if it yields no hit. Presses during that interval are ignored.
Next detection interval (ms)
Sets the minimum time between registered hits, from 100 to 5,000 milliseconds.
Weapon offset (px)
Adjust X and Y to shift the displayed hit position when it consistently differs from the point of aim. Click Reset to zero both offsets.

Click Save Weapon to keep the changes for the selected profile.

Troubleshooting

No camera preview

Check the camera connection, close other apps that may be using the webcam, and click Refresh webcams. Select the camera again and try another available camera mode if the preview stays blank.

The counter misses hits

  1. Check that the preview shows the entire shooting area and that the laser spot is inside the frame.
  2. Turn on Show flares. Increase sensitivity a little at a time and repeat the test. If the background starts lighting up, reduce glare on the target and test again.
  3. If Blob misses a small spot, try Simple and compare the counter after a test pulse.
  4. Wait longer than the Next detection interval between test pulses. With NewSpot, make sure the spot disappears before the next pulse.

The counter increases without a shot

Use Show flares to locate bright reflections or background lights in the preview. Remove them from the frame or lower sensitivity, then fire another test pulse.
