Hit Analyzer 2 Help

Hit Analyzer 2 uses a webcam to detect a laser spot, register hits, and show where they landed on a calibrated target. Open Help or press F1 to return to this guide.

Record your first hit

  1. Connect your webcam and point it at the target so the shooting area is visible in the preview.
  2. Open Camera and select the webcam. If it is missing from the list, click Refresh webcams. Choose a camera mode if the list is available, set the preview orientation, and click Save webcam settings.
  3. Open Weapon and select a profile. Choose FirstSpot for a test with a short laser pulse.
  4. Open Shooting. Clear Show Target Preview to see the camera image during the test. Click Reset hit count to start from zero.
  5. Fire a test pulse at the target. A registered hit increases the Hits counter. If the counter stays at zero, follow the missed hits checklist. To see hits on the target diagram, calibrate the target first.

Calibrate the target

  1. Set the camera, resolution, and flip orientation before calibrating. Keep the camera and target fixed while shooting.
  2. Open Target to display the calibration guides over the live camera image. Click Reset to square if you need a centered starting shape.
  3. Drag the center handle to move the entire guide. Drag the four corner handles until the projected circle follows the outer edge of the physical target and its center matches the bullseye. The guide is yellow when the geometry is valid. If it turns cyan, uncross the edges and spread out the corners until the circle returns.
  4. Click Save target settings to save the calibration together with the current camera settings.
  5. Return to Shooting and enable Show Target Preview. Test a hit at a known location and check where the marker appears.

Check the calibration again after moving the camera or target. Changing the camera, camera mode, or flip resets the active calibration.

Settings reference

Shooting

Reset hit count sets Hits to zero and clears the last hit marker from the target preview. Show Target Preview replaces the camera image with a calibrated target diagram while Shooting is open. The preview choice is saved automatically.

The yellow cross marks the aim position after applying the weapon offset. When the offset moves the aim away from the laser spot, a blue X marks the detected spot. External Event can show the aim path before and after the hit. TimeOut shows the recorded aim path when a hit registers.

Camera

Select a webcam, choose an available resolution and frame rate, and use the flip selector to orient the image. Click Save webcam settings to keep your changes.

Detection

Simple
Looks for the brightest part of the image that meets the detection threshold.
Blob
Uses the center of the largest connected bright region that meets the minimum area.
Sensitivity
Increase sensitivity to pick up dimmer spots. Decrease it if bright areas in the scene are counted as hits.
Brightness, contrast, and saturation
Adjust the camera image that the detector processes. Check the preview and test a pulse after changing these values.
Show flares
Highlights bright regions that meet the detection threshold so you can see which parts of the image may affect detection.
Show Allowed Area
Outlines the allowed area and hatches the margins on the camera image. In TimeOut mode, the final aim position must fall inside this area to register a hit.

Click Save detection settings to keep your changes.

Weapon

The selector displays each profile as its name followed by its filename in parentheses. Profiles are loaded from JSON files in Data/Weapons under the application folder. Selecting a profile applies its saved trigger, interval, and offsets. Click Save Weapon to write the changes to that profile and select it for the next launch.

Next detection interval (ms)
Set a delay of 100 to 5,000 milliseconds. See Trigger types for when the delay starts and how it applies.
Weapon offset (px)
Adjust X and Y from −100 to +100 pixels to align the displayed aim with the point of aim. Positive X moves it right and positive Y moves it up. Click Reset to zero both offsets.

Trigger types

FirstSpot
Registers a hit whenever a spot is detected and the interval since the last hit has elapsed. A spot that stays visible can register repeatedly.
NewSpot
Registers a hit when a spot appears after the interval since the last hit. If it appears too early and stays visible, let it disappear and try again after the interval.
TimeOut
Tracks the spot while you aim. After the spot disappears and the aim path times out, the last position registers if the path spans at least two seconds and the final aim position is inside the allowed area. The aim path times out after 400 milliseconds without a detected spot by default.
External Event
With the main window active, press and hold T long enough for the camera to capture the visible spot. Each accepted press can register one hit. The interval starts at the accepted press, even if it yields no hit. Release T and wait for the interval before pressing again.

Troubleshooting

No camera preview

Check the camera connection, close other apps that may be using the webcam, and click Refresh webcams. Select the camera again and try another available camera mode if the preview stays blank.

The counter misses hits

  1. Check that the preview shows the entire shooting area and that the laser spot is inside the frame.
  2. Turn on Show flares. Increase sensitivity a little at a time and repeat the test. If the background starts lighting up, reduce glare on the target and test again.
  3. If Blob misses a small spot, try Simple and compare the counter after a test pulse.
  4. Check the selected trigger type. With NewSpot, let the spot disappear and wait for the interval before firing again. With TimeOut, turn on Show Allowed Area and check the final aim position. With External Event, check that the main window is active and that the spot is visible while you hold T.

The counter increases without a shot

Use Show flares to locate bright reflections or background lights in the preview. Remove them from the frame or lower sensitivity, then fire another test pulse.

Hits appear in the wrong place on the target

Open Target and check the projected circle and center against the physical target. If the calibration lines up but every hit is shifted in the same direction, check the selected profile's Weapon offset (px).
